    Tune by ear

    Tap a string above to hear its note, then pluck the same string on your ukulele. If yours sounds lower, tighten its peg a little. If it sounds higher, loosen it. Turn only a little at a time. When the two sound the same, switch it on below. New strings stretch, so expect to retune often in the first week.
